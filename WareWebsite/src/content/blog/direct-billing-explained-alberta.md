"Do you direct bill?" is the first question many new clients ask — and for good reason. Paying out of pocket and chasing reimbursement is a hassle. Here's how direct billing actually works, and how to make your first visit seamless.

## What direct billing means

With direct billing, we submit your claim to your insurance company **before you pay**. If your plan covers 80% of a massage, you only pay the remaining 20% at the desk. No receipts to scan, no claim forms, no waiting weeks for reimbursement.

At our Windermere clinic, we direct bill **most major insurance providers** for massage therapy and acupuncture treatments.

## What most Alberta plans cover

Coverage varies by employer and plan, but as a general picture, extended health plans in Alberta commonly include:

- **Massage therapy** performed by a registered massage therapist (RMT) — typically a dollar maximum per year (often $300–$1,000) and sometimes a per-visit cap
- **Acupuncture** performed by a Registered Acupuncturist (R.Ac.) — often a separate annual maximum from massage
- **Manual osteopathy** — included in many, but not all, plans

Because massage and acupuncture usually have **separate annual maximums**, some clients alternate treatments to make the most of their total coverage — a strategy worth asking your therapist about.

## Three things to check on your plan

1. **Does your plan require a doctor's referral?** Most don't, but some plans only reimburse massage with a physician's note on file.
2. **What is your annual maximum, and when does it reset?** Many plans reset January 1 — unused coverage doesn't carry over, so December is a busy month for good reason.
3. **Is there a per-visit cap?** Some plans pay up to a set amount per visit; you cover any difference.

If you're not sure about any of these, call the number on your benefits card — or ask us and we'll help you figure it out.

## What to bring to your first visit

Just your **benefits card** (or the plan and member numbers from your insurer's app) and a piece of ID. We'll set up direct billing on file so every future visit is automatic.

## What about car accident claims?

Treatment after a motor vehicle accident works differently — it's billed through your **MVA insurance claim** rather than your extended health plan.
