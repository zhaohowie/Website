Even a minor fender-bender can leave your body sore in ways that show up days later. Neck stiffness, headaches, shoulder and back pain — these are classic signs of the muscle strain and whiplash that follow a collision. The good news for Albertans: massage therapy for accident injuries can be covered under your **motor vehicle accident (MVA) insurance claim**, separate from your regular health benefits.

## Why soreness shows up late

During a collision, muscles brace hard in a fraction of a second. Adrenaline masks much of the initial discomfort, so many people feel "fine" at the scene and wake up stiff two or three days later. That delayed-onset pattern is normal — but it's a reason to take even small collisions seriously.

## What to do first

1. **Get medically assessed.** See a doctor or clinic soon after the accident — both for your health and because documentation matters for your claim.
2. **Report the accident to your insurer promptly** and open a claim. In Alberta, injuries from an accident should be reported quickly, and treatment is easiest to access when the claim is open early.
3. **Start treatment early.** Once you've been assessed, beginning soft-tissue treatment sooner generally supports a smoother recovery than waiting for pain to "settle on its own."
